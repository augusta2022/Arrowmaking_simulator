Source

Martinón-Torres, M., Li, X. J., Bevan, A., Xia, Y., Zhao, K. & Rehren, Th. “Forty Thousand Arms for a Single Emperor: From Chemical Data to the Labor Organization Behind the Bronze Arrows of the Terracotta Army.” Journal of Archaeological Method and Theory 21 (2014): 534–562. doi:10.1007/s10816-012-9158-z

Note. Crucible loads are separated here by tin and lead plotted against each other, as in the study. The real analyses draw further support from minor elements such as antimony and arsenic, and because unprepared, corroded surfaces had to be measured, the published values are read as trends rather than absolute weight percentages.

Scale is compressed throughout. The numbers are cut down so a class can work both systems in a few minutes.
